    About Me

    With over a decade of expertise in AI and Machine Learning, I am currently serving as a Principal Data Scientist and Technical Lead at Ericsson, Sweden. My professional journey is marked by a passion for innovation, technical leadership, and the development of advanced AI/ML products and tools. I specialize in designing and implementing software architectures for AI-driven solutions, focusing on Generative AI technologies.

    As a strategic technology leader, I have successfully led cross-functional teams and driven the industrialization of complex AI solutions, such as the Agentic RAG-based chatbot at Ericsson. I am dedicated to mentoring junior data scientists, promoting collaboration, and fostering the adoption of cutting-edge AI techniques across the organization.

    My expertise spans:

    • Data Science Leadership: Strategic planning, decision-making, and cross-functional collaboration.
    • Generative AI: RAG techniques, LLM deployment, and prompt engineering.
    • Cloud Platforms: Azure ML, AWS Bedrock, and Google Vertex AI.
    • Programming: Proficient in Python and R.

    I am deeply invested in the fields of Generative AI, Information Retrieval, and Software Development, with a focus on empowering businesses through data-driven innovation. My continuous goal is to explore and implement transformative AI technologies that drive both technical excellence and business value.
